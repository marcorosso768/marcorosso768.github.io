CHANGES MADE
Selected 8 enforcing-contracts series and 191 economies (economies with a region in the source metadata;
regional aggregates excluded; Liechtenstein included although outside the 190-economy ranking);
reshaped to one row per economy; renamed and labelled variables; joined region and income-group metadata.
No imputation. Values unchanged.

SCOPE
Indicators describe a standardized hypothetical commercial dispute in the largest business city
(two cities averaged for 11 economies), based on expert questionnaires. They are not averages of observed cases.
Doing Business was discontinued in September 2021 after data irregularities were found in the 2018 and 2020
editions. Use as historical teaching data. Region and income-group labels are source metadata.
