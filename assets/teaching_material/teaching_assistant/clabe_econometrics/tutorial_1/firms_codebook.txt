firms.csv  -  simulated panel of 800 manufacturing firms observed every year 2019-2023 (4,000 rows)
The data are artificial: they were generated by computer to resemble a business survey.

id         firm identifier (1-800)
year       survey year (2019-2023)
sector     industry: Food, Textiles, Chemicals, Metals, Machinery
region     location of the firm: North, Centre, South
age        years since the firm was founded
va         value added, thousand euros
capital    book value of capital (plant and equipment), thousand euros
employees  number of employees
wage       average hourly labour cost in the firm's province, euros
subsidy    regional hiring incentive in the firm's province, % of the payroll
mgr_educ   years of education of the firm's general manager
rd         1 if the firm carried out research and development during the year
exporter   1 if the firm sold abroad during the year
offered    1 if the firm was offered (at random, in 2021) a place in a public consulting programme
consult    1 if the firm took part in the consulting programme
Note: since 2021, firms located in the South have been eligible for an investment tax credit.
